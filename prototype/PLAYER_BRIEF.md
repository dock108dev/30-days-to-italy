# Player Brief

You are spending a month alone on the Campanian coast. You will hear short Italian lines and type what you want to do or say.

Your goal is to handle the situation—not to produce a perfect sentence.

## You may type

- complete or incomplete Italian;
- a short fragment such as `Due notti`, `Solo questo`, or `Con la carta`;
- Italian structure with an English noun;
- Spanish-influenced Italian;
- `Replay` to hear the normal line again;
- `Slower` to hear the careful-speed line;
- `Transcript` to reveal the exact Italian text;
- a clarification request in any language;
- `Later`, `No thanks`, or another boundary; or
- `Leave` to end the interaction when possible.

Replay, slower audio, and transcripts remain available without penalty. Their use helps distinguish listening from reading; it does not invalidate a practical success.

## Feedback

The facilitator responds to what you meant and applies the practical consequence first.

If your wording could change the money, item, time, location, or promise, the facilitator will clarify. After resolution, a meaning-changing correction appears automatically. A naturalness-only suggestion stays hidden unless you ask for it.

## Your interaction style

You do not need to make small talk, explain why you are alone, or become friendly with anyone. You may finish a transaction and leave. If a character offers optional conversation, you may answer, defer, decline, or ignore it and complete the practical task.

There is no speaking, microphone input, pronunciation evaluation, score, streak, or timer pressure.
