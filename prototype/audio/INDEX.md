# NPC Audio Index

These fixed prototype clips were produced with installed Italian system voices. They are disposable test assets, not approved production performances.

- **Normal:** approximately 190 words per minute.
- **Careful:** approximately 145 words per minute.
- The wording is identical across speeds.
- The player never records audio and no microphone is used.

The facilitator plays normal first. Careful audio is played whenever the player asks for `Slower`. The transcript is shown only after a `Transcript` request.

## Audio integrity rule

If any audio differs from its transcript, sounds corrupt, or fails to play, the facilitator must use the transcript, mark audio unavailable, and exclude that turn from listening evidence. The facilitator must not recreate the line live with different wording during a measured run.
